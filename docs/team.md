# Team & ownership

_Internal — not part of the client-facing deliverable._

We're five Forward Deployed Engineers on this engagement. Split by workstream, then traded
work across those lines so everyone can explain the whole system — the biggest risk on a team
this size is one person building while four people watch.

| Workstream | Owner | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Build (vibe-coded) + deployment | **Randhir** | The LangChain pipeline (`src/`), the Vercel deployment (`app.py`, `index.html`, `vercel.json`), and the README. |
| Discovery & problem selection | _teammate 2_ | `docs/discovery-note.md` — defending the chosen problem against the ranked shortlist. |
| Data & evaluation | _teammate 3_ | `data/generate_mock_data.py`, the evaluator rubric in `src/chains.py`, and the failure case shown live. |
| Frontend & demo | _teammate 4_ | `index.html` polish and the live-demo script/flow for the pitch. |
| Presentation & client Q&A prep | _teammate 5_ | The 20-minute pitch structure, cost-line framing, "what we'd build next." |

Fill in the four names above. Every member still presents at least one segment live, and every
member needs to be able to answer questions about the whole system, not just their line.
